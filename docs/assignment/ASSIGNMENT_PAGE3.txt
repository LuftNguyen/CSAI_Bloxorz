2.4    Victory
A stage is won when the block falls into the goal hole. This happens only when the block is
standing upright on the goal cell. A block that merely lies across the goal cell does not win.


2.5    Advanced tiles
Each stage may include the following special tiles. These tiles introduce additional gameplay
mechanics by altering how the block interacts with the board.

   • Fragile tiles: A fragile (orange) tile can support the block only when its weight is distributed
     (the block is lying). If the block stands on a fragile tile, the tile breaks and the block falls.
     The block may safely lie across fragile tiles.

   • Bridges: A bridge is a tile whose passability is controlled by a switch. A closed bridge
     behaves like empty space, while an open bridge behaves like a normal floor tile. Each switch
     is linked to one or more bridge tiles.

The game features three types of switches:

   • Soft switch (octagon / circle): Activated when any part of the block presses it, regardless
     of orientation (standing or lying).

   • Heavy switch (X-shaped): Activated only when the block is standing on it; lying on it
     does not trigger the switch.

   • Split switch (bracket-shaped "( )"): Activated only when the block is standing on it.
     It teleports the block to predefined positions and splits it into two 1 × 1 × 1 cubes.

When activated, a switch affects its linked bridges in one of two ways: toggle (each activation
switches the bridges between open and closed states) or permanent (the bridges are set to a fixed
state after activation).

Block splitting. After being split into two unit cubes, they are controlled one at a time (e.g.,
switching control with the spacebar) and merge back into a standard 1×1×2 block when positioned
on adjacent tiles. A single cube can activate soft switches but cannot activate heavy switches or
complete the stage.
